Your ledger stays on your device

Ledglet is a local-first Android personal finance ledger. It stores the records you enter or import, such as Accounts, Categories, people, amounts, currencies, dates, comments, financial history and exchange-rate evidence. The production database is encrypted; its key is protected using Android Keystore. This does not protect information visible on an unlocked device or copies you choose to share.

The current app has no app login, advertising, analytics SDK or automatic remote crash-reporting service. It does not upload your ledger to Ledglet servers. Local-first does not mean that the app never uses the internet: exchange-rate requests, purchase verification and your external actions are described below. Demo uses a separate fictional local dataset.

Optional notification access

Manual ledger use does not require notification access. Before opening Android's notification-access settings, Ledglet explains the feature and asks you to activate access explicitly. Android then controls the grant.

With access enabled, Android can deliver notifications to Ledglet, including while its screen is closed. The listener reads source-app identity, notification title/text and delivery metadata. It records detected app names, package names and last-seen times so you can choose sources. Detection is not monitoring: notification bodies from apps you have not enabled do not become stored captures or financial records.

For enabled sources, money-relevant notifications and matching rules can produce locally stored captures, review items or automatic ledger postings. Automatic posting requires an enabled, successfully previewed Auto-create rule and valid financial configuration. Reconnecting the listener or enabling a source can also process notifications still present in the Android tray. Parsing, matching and recovery run on-device; notification text is not sent to a parsing or analytics service.

You can change source monitoring, pause capture, disable rules, or revoke access in Android settings. Revocation stops new Android notification delivery; it does not erase already received data or undo ledger postings. Work already received can finish. Manual entries, history and corrections remain available whether you decline or revoke access.

What is retained, and how to remove it

Stored captures can contain original notification titles and bodies, source identity, timestamps, processing outcomes and links to rules or posted Operations. Rules can retain example notification text separately.

Resolved captures (posted, finalized, ignored or duplicate) become eligible for cleanup more than 30 days after their last update. Cleanup runs during capture processing or workspace loading, not on a guaranteed daily schedule. Unresolved, ready-for-processing and error captures are not deleted merely because they are old. You can delete visible capture items. Deleting a capture does not delete a rule's saved example or the associated financial history. Delete rules separately to remove their saved examples; archiving a rule retains it.

Local recovery identifiers and deletion markers can remain after a capture is removed to prevent duplicate replay. Their cleanup follows recovery state, not the capture's 30-day rule. Financial records and correction history are retained while the app's data remains; editing or deleting a posted entry uses correction records rather than erasing its history.

Clearing Ledglet's app storage or uninstalling removes its app-owned local data. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Encrypted backups you explicitly export can include financial records, retained notifications, rule examples and recovery metadata. Exported files remain in the location you choose until you remove them there. A chosen storage provider may synchronize those files under its own settings; app-data deletion does not remove them.

Internet exchange-rate requests

Production currency conversion and budget planning can request rates from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.app, api.frankfurter.dev) and the National Bank of Ukraine (bank.gov.ua). Requests contain currency codes and a requested rate date, or use a latest-rate fallback. They do not contain your transaction amounts, balances, names, notification text, encryption keys or backup contents.

The provider and network infrastructure receive ordinary connection metadata, including your IP address and request timing. Their own processing and retention are outside Ledglet's local-storage controls. Requests use HTTPS; available persisted rates and local fallbacks support offline work.

Google Play purchases and promo restoration

In versions with Play billing enabled, Google Play handles payments, promo redemption and purchase receipts. Ledglet can check for completed purchases when you open the app or restore Pro. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app user identifier, app and device information, product and purchase receipt information, and connection metadata such as an IP address to verify purchase ownership, support restoration and process purchase reports. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

RevenueCat uses the IP address from the most recent app request to estimate your country for reporting and analytics. After determining the country, RevenueCat discards the IP address and keeps the country in the record associated with the app user identifier. This can happen when Ledglet loads the Pro price, even if you do not make a purchase.

In versions with direct promo verification enabled, the app sends the Google Play purchase token, a Firebase App Check token and a request identifier over HTTPS to a verifier hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The verifier uses Google Play to confirm the purchase and check for changes to the same purchase so Ledglet can grant or restore Permanent Pro and keep access in sync with the purchase. Cloudflare can process connection metadata, such as your IP address, when it delivers and protects the service. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to check that the request comes from an eligible Ledglet installation. Google processes app and signing information, Play license status, device integrity signals and Firebase SDK context for this check. Google Privacy Policy

Ledglet stores an ownership record on your device separately from the ledger. Removing the app removes that local record. Purchase history held by Google or RevenueCat can remain. For purchase data questions or requests, contact support@inwestman.uk.

Pro Preview

In versions with Pro Preview enabled, Ledglet hashes an identifier specific to the app and your Android profile on your device. To start or recover a Preview, the app sends the hashed identifier, a Google Play Integrity token and a request identifier over HTTPS to the Ledglet service on Cloudflare Workers. The service checks the token with Google to verify the app and its Play license.

Cloudflare D1 stores a hash derived from that identifier using a server key, together with the original start and end dates. The service retains this record after expiry and across reinstalls so it can return the same dates and enforce one Preview per Android profile. For questions or requests about Preview data, contact support@inwestman.uk.

Feedback and support

Ordinary app diagnostics use local stage/state, error categories and identifiers, not raw notification bodies, financial values, passphrases, keys or backup contents. There is no automatic support-data upload. System feedback omits financial details by default; explicitly enabling financial-details feedback can make those details visible in Android notifications, subject to your device and lock-screen settings.

Contact Ledglet support at support@inwestman.uk. If you send an email, your email service, the services handling this support address and the support recipient process your email address, message and any attachments you choose. Email providers may retain messages under their own policies. Support uses the information you send to respond and investigate your report. The app does not grant support access to your local ledger.

Start with the installed version, Android version, steps to reproduce and a sanitized description. Do not send financial records, raw notifications, passwords, keys or backups. Check screenshots and attachments before sending. Contact the same address to request deletion of a support message; deleting app data cannot delete email or copies held by an email provider. No fixed support-email retention or deletion deadline is promised.

Visiting this policy
